Hom Nuea

🛍️ Products & Services HOM NUEA is a Northern Thai fusion fast-food brand. · Its menu combines traditional Northern Thai flavors with modern street food. · Customers can customize their meals by choosing dough, meat, vegetables, and sauces. · The business operates restaurants in shopping malls and automated vending machines at BTS and MRT stations.

📜 History & Milestones HOM NUEA was established around the concept of bringing traditional Northern Thai cuisine to modern urban consumers. · The introduction of vending machines at BTS and MRT stations marked an important milestone in expanding its sales channels. · These vending machines made HOM NUEA’s products more accessible to commuters and people with busy lifestyles. · In Year 4, the company increased its social media marketing efforts, helping sales recover significantly.

🎯 Goals Expand the business to CentralWorld next year. · Increase brand awareness among urban consumers. · Improve sales performance through digital marketing and convenient distribution channels.

👥 Customers & Markets HOM NUEA targets teenagers, students, and young adults aged 16–35. · Its key customers include office workers, commuters, and people seeking convenient meals. · The brand appeals to customers who enjoy local Thai flavors presented in a modern format. · Its main markets include shopping malls and busy BTS and MRT stations.

✨ What Makes It Different Combines authentic Northern Thai flavors with the convenience of modern fast food. · Offers customizable meals to suit different customer preferences. · Sources ingredients directly from local suppliers to support local communities. · Uses eco-friendly packaging to reduce environmental impact. · Offers convenient purchasing options through restaurants and vending machines.

📈 Numbers That Changed Year 1: The overall sales index stood at 100. · Year 2: The sales index increased to 125 following the introduction of vending machines. · Year 3: The sales index decreased slightly to 120. · Year 4: The sales index recovered to 168 following increased investment in social media marketing. · Next Year: The sales index is projected to reach 210.
